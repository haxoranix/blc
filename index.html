Our Process

A clear path from first call to final walkthrough.

Construction can feel overwhelming, but the right process keeps every step organized. Black Lion Construction guides your project with clear planning, consistent communication, and careful coordination from start to finish.

01

Consultation

We discuss your goals, ideas, budget, and expectations while reviewing the space and project needs.

02

Project Planning & Estimate

We define the scope, outline materials, labor, timelines, and prepare a clear project estimate.

03

Design & Material Selections

We help coordinate layouts, finishes, fixtures, colors, materials, and key design decisions.

04

Scheduling & Preparation

We organize scheduling, permitting, material ordering, site preparation, and project logistics.

05

Construction Phase

Our team manages the construction work with professionalism, communication, cleanliness, and attention to detail.

06

Final Walkthrough

We review the finished project with you and make sure the details meet expectations and our quality standards.

07

Project Completion

We complete the project with a focus on quality, durability, and a positive client experience from start to finish.
